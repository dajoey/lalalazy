About the data

Where the numbers come from. Boards, spaces, branches, every enemy's weakness, star ratings, vulnerabilities and panel casts are read from the game's own Crucible tables (patch 7.56). Nothing here is typed in by hand, so a balance patch means a regeneration, not a rewrite.

How the picks are made. The familiar recommendations use the same advisor that drives the LazyCrucible plugin's selection screens, verified in real runs: weakness matches on the fight's main enemy count most, then crowd control the enemy is actually vulnerable to, then an unanswered interrupt, dispel or cleanse, then the beast's main stat and constitution at the board's rank sync. The third horn is deliberately traded toward utility rather than a third weakness hit, the way the good guides do it. Picks are suggestions with their reasons shown; your run, your call.

What this does not do. It does not play the board for you, does not need any plugin, and does not track your character. Your roster lives in this browser only.

Credits. Guides that shaped the fight notes and the advisor's rules: Icy Veins, the FFXIV Community Wiki, Eorzean Tavern, and the Japanese board guides at nettoge and asellog. Score bonuses are listed here; for full score planning see Evyx's calculator.
