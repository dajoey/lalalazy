Lazy Fish Sitter

Original Testing Config window

Sits you down while you fish. That's it.

Sit while you cast
Checks every few seconds while you are fishing; if you are standing, it runs /sit for you.
Stays out of the way
Never re-sits once you are seated — ground sit, chair, or pose — and pauses in cutscenes, events and combat.
