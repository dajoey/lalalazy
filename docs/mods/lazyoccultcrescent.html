Lazy Occult Crescent

Fork Active Config window

Knows the whole Crescent. Both horns.

North Horn, day one
FATE and Critical Encounter tables datamined straight from patch 7.55, plus all six North Horn aetheryte shards and every one of the eight new phantom jobs.
Learns the zone itself
Shard and event positions live in the map layout, not the game’s data sheets — so instead of hand-surveying them, the plugin reads them off the live object table and remembers. A new zone bootstraps over your first lap.
Treasure & carrot radar
Draws lines to nearby coffers and Fortune Carrots, with a precomputed optimal looting route so you stop backtracking across the map.
Live FATE & CE tracking
What’s up, how far along it is, when it’ll finish, and what it drops — with one-click teleport and pathing to it.
Drives Gluttony Combo
Picks up GluttonyCombo for rotations when it’s installed, ahead of Wrath — currently the only provider that implements the 7.55 phantom jobs.
Trackers & automation
Silver/gold and EXP per hour, phantom job buff automation, and an optional hands-off loop for FATEs, critical encounters and mob farming.
