Lazy Market Companion

Original Testing Config window

Your always-sell list, listed and priced while you sleep.

Auto-Market list
Items you always sell, each with its own stack size, keep-in-bags reserve and per-retainer cap. Add from your bags with a right-click.
Bags and retainer loot
Lists from your inventory and from the retainer's own venture haul, in the order you choose.
Match, never undercut
Every new listing is priced at the current lowest board price (or Universalis). Existing listings get re-priced too.
Runs inside AutoRetainer
Optionally hooks AutoRetainer's venture cycle so each retainer gets stocked and priced with no clicks from you.
