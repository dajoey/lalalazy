LazyFoodBuff

Original Active Config window

Never forget to eat your food again.

Auto-eat in duties
Eats food when you enter combat duties — dungeons, raids, trials, alliance raids, criterion and variant.
Best food, per job
Auto-selects the highest-value food in your bag for your current job, or set a manual per-job pick.
Refresh & warn
Re-eats before the buff expires and warns you in chat when you are running low on food.

Install — add the repo, then grab LazyFoodBuff
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dajoey/lalalazy/main/pluginmaster.json
1. /xlsettings → Experimental → paste under Custom Plugin Repositories.
2. Save, open /xlplugins, search LazyFoodBuff.
3. Install — done.
