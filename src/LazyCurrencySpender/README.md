# Lazy Currency Spender — Optimal FFXIV Currency Exchanges

A premium currency exchange helper for FFXIV. Lazy Currency Spender connects with Universalis pricing data and your character sheet to identify the absolute best ways to spend Poetics, weekly tomestones, scrips, and other capped/uncapped currencies, maximizing your profit margins and completing missing collectibles.

---

## 🌟 Core Features

* **Universalis Integration:** Connects to real-time Universalis API endpoints to pull current market board pricing for items purchasable with your currencies, showing you the exact gil-per-currency ratio.
* **Tomestone Gear & Equipment Exchange UI:** Special support for weekly capped and uncapped endgame tomestones. Displays non-tradable equipment and weapons (like Bygone Brass gear) and translates exchange choices clearly in the UI.
* **Collectibles Integration:** Scans your character sheet to identify missing minions, mounts, orchestrion rolls, and other collectibles that are purchasable with your current currencies, prioritizing them so you can finish your collections.

---

## 🚀 Installation

Add the custom plugin repository URL to your Dalamud settings:

```text
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dajoey/lalalazy/main/pluginmaster.json
```

1. In-game, type `/xlsettings` in chat to open Dalamud Settings.
2. Select the **Experimental** tab.
3. Scroll to **Custom Plugin Repositories**, paste the repository URL into the empty field, and click **+**.
4. Click **Save and Close** (bottom-right).
5. Open `/xlplugins` in chat, search for **Lazy Currency Spender** in the **Available Plugins** tab, and click **Install**.
